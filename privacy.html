Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Purpose

Proxmox Backups is a personal application used to transfer backup files from a private Proxmox server to Google Drive using rclone.

Google Drive Access

The application requests the Google Drive drive.file scope, limiting access to files created or explicitly authorized for the application.

Data Storage and Sharing

Backup files are stored in the account owner's Google Drive. The application does not sell or share data with third parties. No backup data is stored on this public website.

Data Retention and Deletion

Backup files remain in Google Drive until deleted by the account owner. Access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the application owner through the contact information displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
